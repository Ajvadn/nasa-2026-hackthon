How to read mission readiness

This is a learning simulation. The unit labels are real, but the component values and scoring rules are simplified game estimates, not NASA flight hardware specifications or a validated engineering model.

MassBus + payload. Above 85% of launcher capacity: −9; over capacity: −16 to −30.
PowerInstrument demand vs bus supply. Above 80%: −7; over supply: −17 to −30.
BudgetFixed game budget less bus, payload, launcher, and orbit. Under $100M remaining: −8; overspend: −17 to −30.
Data linkGenerated data vs communications capacity. Above 85%: −5; over capacity: −12 to −20.
Science fitWrong destination orbit: −26. Each missing mission target: −11. Empty payload: −25.
Flight resultReadiness + pilot points − 0.55 × flight risk, clamped to 0–100.

Use the score to compare choices inside ORBITAL. For real mission design, consult mission requirements and verified engineering analyses.
